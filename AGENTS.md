## Research onboarding

Before changing Perquire's scientific mechanism, read:

1. docs/research_contract.md
2. docs/experiments/causal_feedback_v2_preregistration.md
3. franklinbaldo/papers/research/semantic-systems-map.md
4. franklinbaldo/papers/research/semantic-systems-evidence-state.md

Do not restart the project from zero. The v1 adaptive-scaling study already failed its frozen operational-validity gate; do not rerun or reinterpret it as if that were unknown.

Do not import evidence from Pontifex, Torus, or Semantic Atlas into Perquire's local claims. Those projects are adjacent observation channels, not substitute evidence.

The active scientific question is causal-feedback v2: whether true target-relevant feedback improves search trajectories over prospectively paired decoy and null feedback under matched proposer/resources.

A positive optimizer-space result is not semantic recovery. Stronger semantic claims require a separately frozen held-out evaluator.

## Experiment discipline

- Preserve frozen protocol boundaries.
- Keep target-free substrate qualification separate from target-aware scientific evaluation.
- Never use test-target outcomes to choose provider, prompts, stopping, budget, model family, or rerun policy.
- Report invalid/failing cells; do not survivor-filter them into a clean positive result.
- Prefer a direct discriminant over generic skepticism.
- Before proposing a new mechanism, identify which existing negative or failed gate it is meant to explain.
- For cross-project comparison, use the Unified Semantic Identification Benchmark contract in franklinbaldo/papers and preserve its assembly/student/val/test separation.

## Cross-project posture

Perquire, Pontifex, Semantic Atlas, and Semantic Observers address different observation modes.

Perquire asks what can be inferred about an unknown representation through iterative candidate generation and scalar feedback.

If a future result suggests a shared mechanism with Pontifex or Atlas, treat that as a bridge hypothesis to test under the unified benchmark, not as automatic evidence transfer.
